## Question

Given the venue inventory, the settled claims, and the readiness bar — which venues are used, in what
order, and what does each one need?

This is the plan the `/to-tickets` breakdown executes, and the last decision on the route.

Decide:

- Which venues are in and which are dropped, with the reason. A venue ruled out here is worth recording
  so it is not reconsidered from scratch.
- The order. With one project and no audience, venues are not equivalent and mistakes are not
  independent: a weak first showing in the highest-leverage venue cannot be retried, while a registry
  submission is nearly free and reversible. Decide what goes first, what waits, and what is a
  one-shot that should go last once the pitch has survived contact elsewhere.
- What artifact each venue needs, referencing the settled claims rather than restating them — a registry
  entry, a post, a comment, a README section are different lengths of the same claim.
- The trigger for each step: what must have happened before the next one fires, so the sequence is a
  plan rather than a list.
- What is deliberately *not* done. Paid distribution is out of scope; decide whether anything else that
  looks obvious is being skipped on purpose.

The output is a decided sequence, ready to hand off. Nothing is posted from this ticket.
