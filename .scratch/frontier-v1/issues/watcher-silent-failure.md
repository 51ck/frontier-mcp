## Problem

Found while answering [[T59]]. Two paths leave a driver serving a stale scan with no sign of it:

- On macOS, past 4096 recursive watches in one process every watch emits `EMFILE`. The error handler closes the watcher and nothing re-attaches.
- On Linux, a failed `watch()` on one directory is swallowed, so that directory is unwatched. The budget is `max_user_watches`, shared with the user's editors.

A third, smaller one: attaching a new workspace rebuilds the FSEvents stream, and a write landing on an already-watched workspace at that instant was not seen within 800ms in 2 of 48 tries (0 of 144 at 1ms or later). The settle invalidations cover only the newly attached watch.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A driver whose watcher closed on error, or could not watch a directory, says so in the Board's warnings
- [ ] Such a driver stops trusting its cached scan, or re-attaches; which one is decided and stated
- [ ] Whether attaching one workspace invalidates the others is decided, with the loss re-measured on a larger sample
