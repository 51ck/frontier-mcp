## Problem

Edges can be followed in one direction only. A Ticket's header shows `blocked_by`; nothing shows which Tickets name *it*. "What does resolving this unblock?" has no route short of reading every Board and scanning `blocked_by=` fields by eye.

Capture, 2026-11-13:

- After resolving `T99` during an unattended run, the consumer re-read Boards to learn whether anything had become takeable. The `update_ticket` result said `T99 updated` and nothing about the graph.
- Choosing among several takeable Tickets, the one that unblocks the most is the better pick, and that number is not visible anywhere.
- The research doc measured that neither the `get_tickets` nor the `update_ticket` module renders dependents, that 49 of 116 Tickets here have at least one, and that the most any has is 4 — so the answer is always short.

This is a graph query off the cached scan, not a text search. It needs no new argument and does not depend on `Tik4z0t`.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** `get_tickets` shows what a Ticket blocks, and a `resolve` result names what it made takeable.

**Current behavior:**
Edges render forward only. Checked at triage: the `get_tickets` header carries `blocked_by` and nothing about dependents, and an `update_ticket` result is the id, `updated`, and the Status line.

**Desired behavior:**
- The `get_tickets` header gains `blocks=` listing the Tickets that name this one in `blocked_by`, omitted when there are none. A dependent in another Effort renders `T9@beta`, as a foreign blocker does.
- A `resolve` result gains one line naming the Tickets that this resolve made takeable, omitted when there are none. A dependent still held by another unresolved blocker is not named.
- A `drop` result names the dependents it orphaned, on the same one-line form.
- Both lists are capped at ten ids with the rest counted.

**Key interfaces:**
- The Frontier computation, run before and after the write for the takeable diff, or its equivalent
- The `get_tickets` header rendering and the `update_ticket` result rendering
- No input schema changes

**Acceptance criteria:**
The seven in the body stand. For the fifth, the decision is made here: `drop` reports the dependents it orphaned.

**Out of scope:**
- Reverse Edges on the Board line
- Prose mentions of a Ticket id; only `blocked_by` counts
- Any new argument
