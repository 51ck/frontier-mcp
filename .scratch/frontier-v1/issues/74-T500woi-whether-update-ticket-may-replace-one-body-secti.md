## Question

Should `update_ticket` be able to replace a single `##` section of a Ticket body, named by its heading, up to the next `##`?

Cases that cannot be expressed today:

- A Ticket filed with a claim marked "inferred, not tested" is later tested, and its Problem section should say what was found. This happened to `Tlgyn13` on the day it was filed; the result went into a comment that contradicts the body.
- A criterion is worded wrongly and should be reworded or removed.

## Open points

- May the replace target the answer section, which `resolve.answer` already owns?
- May it target the comment log, which is append-only by convention and stored byte-for-byte?
- May it target the acceptance criteria, and if so what happens to boxes already ticked?
- Is a value containing a `##` line refused on the rule `edit_map` applies (T96)?
- Is a section-level replace the right primitive at all, against a literal find-and-replace within the body?
- Whole-body replace is not wanted: it costs the most tokens, can clobber the comment log and ticked boxes, and widens every fix into a race.
